Who authorises a transfer

coop-api holds no authority. It owns the intent, resolves which authority is required, and records the outcome. The authority itself always lives in a key, a role, or a vote.

AuthorityThree classes — one signature object, three ways to grant it

Scoped key

“the system observed this”

Caps, recipient allowlist, expiry, rate limit. The only class that runs unattended.

Role holder

“I approve this”

Bounded by the role's grant matrix. A treasurer approves payments, not treasury ops.

Group vote

“we accept this”

The authority of last resort. Most expensive — reaches for it rarely.

Use the cheapest class that can honestly attest. A routine payment needs a scoped key; a disputed one needs a role holder; a batch nobody witnessed needs a vote.
