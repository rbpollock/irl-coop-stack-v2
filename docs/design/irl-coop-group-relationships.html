irl.coop — Group Relationships & the Safe

Declarative model: the Safe primitive, the four relationship dimensions, and how a co-op and a nonprofit map onto them

The Safe — universal account

  • • Owner set + threshold (N-of-M), modules, vault, registry entry
  • • Every actor is a Safe: person = 1-of-1 · group = N-of-M · DAO
  • • Ownership implies membership ONLY — nothing else

Relationships are declared

  • • Four dimensions: governance · infrastructure · trust · economics
  • • member-of · subgroup-of · federated-with · sponsored-by
  • • Registry is the source of truth; provisioning derives from it

One model, two shapes

  • • Co-op: flat seats, autonomous subgroups, federation membership
  • • Nonprofit: nested control, reserved powers, sponsorship
  • • Members can sit in many Safes — a graph, not a tree

One big world

  • • Context is derived from the action — no hat-switching
  • • Unified approval inbox across all of a user's Safes
  • • Roles = session keys + guards (access, not ownership)
  • • Relationship-scoped ledgers for barter & labor swaps

zk-badges

  • • Verifiable participation — prove it, reveal nothing
  • • Guard-enforced gates (quests, priority, tenure, votes)
  • • Holder-bound + decaying (no farming, no sale)
  • • Money stays auditable — ZK never touches cash flows
