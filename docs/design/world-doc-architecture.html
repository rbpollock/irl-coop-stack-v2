irl.coop World-Doc Architecture

Projection, not store of record — every user's "what I can see and do", derived & filtered

Boundary rule

  • • World-doc = what I can see and do
  • • Stores of record = what is true
  • • Rebuildable · migratable · filtered
  • • Never keys, blobs, or ephemera

Contacts & privacy

  • • Derived from group membership
  • • + small explicit, user-owned edges
  • • Group tiers: open / members / hidden
  • • Hidden = provable, not enumerable (zk)

Seats & finance

  • • Seat = membership + persona (alias/roles)
  • • One persona rendered everywhere
  • • Finance = proofs, not balances
  • • Pending approvals · timelocks · entitlements
