- KC 25.0.6 is REQUIRED: the WebAuthn `authenticate()` got the ID-less
  (null-user) guard in 25; 24.x NPEs on the login-page GET.
- `conditional-user-configured` NPEs on the webauthn's `configuredFor` in
  EVERY version (checked 24.0.4/24.0.5/25/26 source) — the
  `browser-passkey` flow uses the built-in browser COPY + a FLAT
  `webauthn-authenticator-passwordless` (ALTERNATIVE) at the top. Never add
  the condition. Recipe: `infra/scripts/kc-webauthn-config.py`.
- e2e-test is ENROLLED (the required action resolved). The files-flow E2E
  login: full-kit sign-in → "Sign in with a passkey" → KC username+password
  (the secondary mechanism; Google for members) → ceremony only on first
  login. A DIRECT gateway authorize bypasses NextAuth state/csrf and fails
  the OAuth callback — always go through the sign-in page.
- The coop-api wants the Bearer JWT (the page's `/api/auth/session`
  endpoint exposes it) — not the coop_session cookie (401 invalid_token).
