The pact in one chart: the same 40 members, the same $4,000. In the rented stack 82¢ of every dollar exits to rent before the work happens. In the cooperative ~98¢ stays in the loop. The cost isn't what cooperation is — it's how much of their own money the cooperative stack lets the community keep.
