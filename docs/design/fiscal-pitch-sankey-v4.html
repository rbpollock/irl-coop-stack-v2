Where a community dollar goes

Every $1 a member-run organization raises — where it comes from, the functions it must carry to run, and what finally reaches the community.

Organized by function — the nonprofit budget lens (a Form 990 classifies expense as management/general, program, and fundraising; we show the same four functions a member-run org actually carries). Amounts are illustrative placeholders modeled on the categories in docs/design/cost-model.data.json (verified 2026-09-13) — a draft for the coop to swap. Community impact = 100 − running costs, so the receipt balances. Hover a bar or flow to trace a dollar.
