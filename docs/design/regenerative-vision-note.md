# Design Note: Regenerative Score as a ZK-Metric

Status: draft (vision record). Author: Robbie + Hermes, Aug 2026.

## The Vision

To build and foster a global network of locally-governed, regenerative, skill-sharing communities. The technology stack underpins this by providing individuals and cooperatives with mathematical sovereignty, cooperative ownership primitives, and uncorrelatable identity.

## ZK-Metric: Regenerative Score

To measure and align participation inside and across regenerative communities without exposing raw individual transactions or private data, the stack will incorporate a **Regenerative Score** as a core Zero-Knowledge (ZK) metric.

### Principles

1. **Privacy-Preserving**: Proof of cooperative acts, skill-sharing, and regenerative land/coalition contributions are computed locally on-device. Only the verified cryptographic proof of the score is submitted or verified on-chain (using ZK-SNARKs/ZK-proofs).
2. **Individual & Group Metrics**: Both individual members and entire groups (such as local farming coalitions or tool libraries) maintain their respective scores, proving trust and community contribution without linking them to physical tracking.
3. **Sovereign Reputation**: This score functions as a decentralized, non-transferable primitive for governance weights, resource allocation, or micro-grant distribution.
