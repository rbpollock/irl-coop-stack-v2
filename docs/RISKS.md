# RISKS.md — risk register

Status: checkpoint register · 2026-09-29.
Impact and likelihood are the maintainer's judgement from repository evidence,
not measured. "Hibernation action" is what should be done *before* the gap, not
after — after two years, some of these stop being mitigable at all.

| Risk | Category | Impact | Likelihood | Evidence | Current mitigation | Required proof or decision | Hibernation action |

| **R-01 Bus factor = 1.** One person, one host, one GitHub account, no co-steward. | Organizational | High | Certain | `AGENTS.md`; `git log` — single author; `origin` = a personal account; owner: no collaborators | Documentation only | D-29: name a successor or custodian | Ask the owner to name at least one recovery contact |

| **R-11 Financial / treasury misuse.** | Financial | High | Low today | The payment path is **wired, and has been attempted 4 times — every attempt failed**: 4 `payment_intent` rows, all status `failed`, and 23 `rail_event` rows, the last on 2026-09-16. Credentials ARE provisioned (`PEER_PAY_API_KEY` and `PEER_PAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` resolve from the vault; `payments.rail-token` is derived), and the chain is pinned to Base `8453`. What is absent is deployment and custody, not code or secrets. `src/payments.ts` plus a non-custodial `CoopUsdcRouter` (with a test file and a deploy script) plus a Peer.xyz rail at `:3010`; `apps/coop-api.yaml` pins a destination address and `PAYMENTS_CHAIN_ID: 8453` (Base). Were a real payment initiated, money would move on **Base mainnet** | Rail credentials are provisioned and the destination is set, so the rail is live; the router is NOT deployed (no address recorded anywhere, `~/.secrets` absent) and no transfer has ever completed — the 4 failures are undiagnosed | **Who holds the keys to the pinned destination address** — not recorded anywhere; then a risk-bounded first transfer; and a backup (R-07) must exist first | Do not enable a real payment until the destination's key custody is written down and a backup exists |
